Agent Concept: Gild 
This agent is similar to Veto but I think there is still room for more than one utility intercepting agent. With Veto’s pickrate so low, maybe there is an alternate take that would make players use it more.
Veto's Interceptor removes value from the round. Gild's Catcher moves it from one team to the other, and makes you pay a positional cost to collect. Anti-utility as theft rather than denial, with the counterplay built into the item leading the enemy to the player.


Identity:
A futuristic trapper and survivalist with an elegant, high-tech aesthetic. Their body and equipment are made of gold and tinted/smoky glass. Could also have a hunters cloak for a unique silhouette
A refined aristocratic hunter who treats the battlefield as a playground. Fascinated by the elegance of nature, she developed golden drones that mimic creatures, allowing them to capture and preserve enemy technology as trophies. 
Beautiful and patient, and Obsessed with collecting.


She is actually being piloted remotely by her owner/operator back in England, so a lot of her personality leaks through. (Could have some fun internal dialogue/fighting with the bots mind)

Core Gameplay
intercepts thrown projectiles and can use them once collected
Catch -> Decide -> Risk the retrieval -> Repurpose the enemy's utility.
probably first contact on defense (with late round suvivabilty), and a mid round lurker on offense


Abilities V1 (Sentinel):
mini demo vid on youtube
E: Catcher [3 charges, 150 each]
* A floating chandelier-like drone that creates a trigger area. Made of golden moths
* Captures up to 3 thrown enemy projectiles.
* Captured utility is stored inside the Catcher rather than immediately added to the player.
* The player can recall the Catcher, causing its drones to physically fly back to them and transfer the captured utility into their inventory.
* The recall visibly/sound-wise reveals the player's position, creating risk.
* Aternatively, the player can release/detonate the captured item at the Catcher's location, sacrificing the ability to retrieve it.


When an object is caught the moth will float above it contained in a glass sphere


Q: Tinted Lens [2 charges, 200 each] 
A specialized lens that temporarily reduces the duration of incoming flashes and stuns
C: Internal Research [2 charges, 250 each]
Absorb a caught grenade as a power source. your next bullet does 1.5x damage
* bait the players to chase the drone, then deal massive damage in an ambush
* only lasts 15 seconds


X: Drone Swarm [6 Ult Points]
Deploys a large swarm over an area, creating a much larger version of the Catcher's interception zone.
* Intercepts and captures thrown enemy projectiles within the area.
* Captured utility can either be recalled to the player or released/detonated at its current location.
* Creates a high-risk/high-reward choice between safely using stolen utility in place or exposing your position to retrieve it.
* Placed with a tablet like a brim smoke (no line of sight needed)


Version 1 Postmortem
1. Drone swarm is the boring part of the kit, maybe lean into the big game hunter aesthetic.
2. Also I think that tinted lens is too strong since it makes most characters offenses useless
3. Doing extra damage with internal research won't be known by the targets until they die
