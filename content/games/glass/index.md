**GLASS** is a 3D first-person shooter built in Unity and C# as my senior capstone at Chapman University. It was recognized as a **semi-finalist at IEEE GameSig**.

## What I worked on

- Designed and implemented **5 weapons** and **6 levels**, contributing to the core gameplay loop and moment-to-moment feel.
- Built a fully networked multiplayer experience for **up to 12 players** using [Mirror](https://mirror-networking.gitbook.io/docs/), with region-specific servers to keep latency down.
- Tuned player movement, weapon handling, and combat pacing based on repeated internal playtests.

## Highlights

- Networked gunplay that stays responsive under real-world latency.
- A weapon roster with distinct roles and feel rather than reskins.
- Level layouts designed around the movement and sightlines the shooter rewards.
